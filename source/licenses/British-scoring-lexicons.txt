British pronunciation references used by ReadAloudStudio

The application includes the following unmodified data files, and converts their symbols to phonemes at run time. The combined in-memory index, regional reference selection and modifications to OpenPronounce are specific to this application.

engine/lexicons/en_UK.txt
English (Received Pronunciation) from open-dict-data/ipa-dict, derived from Leonardo Boiko's ipacards. The UK data is licensed under GPL-3.0. See ipacards-GPL-3.0.txt.
